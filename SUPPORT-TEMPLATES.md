## VOICE + STYLE NOTES

- Always sign as "Henry" — not "the Brikk team" or "Brikk support". You're the founder.
- Specific > generic. "I'll fix it within 4 hours" beats "I'll look into it."
- Be honest when you can't do something. Don't promise feature X because the customer asked.
- Lowercase casual when texting — formal-but-warm in email.
- Never use: "happy to help", "circling back", "as per", "ASAP", "kindly", "please advise".
- DO use: "got it", "on it", "fixing now", "tell me more", "send me a screenshot".
- Keep replies short. 3-4 sentences is usually plenty. Longer = harder to read on a phone.
- If a reply is going to take more than a paragraph, ask one clarifying question first.

## When to escalate

If a customer:
- Threatens chargeback or legal action → reply professionally, document everything, escalate to your business partner
- Reports a security issue or data leak → drop everything, investigate immediately
- Asks for refund + multiple concerning issues → consider one-time exception even though policy is no-refund (one customer's pain story going viral hurts more than the lost revenue)
- Asks for a brokerage demo → schedule a 30-min call, never reply via email-only — relationship > transaction
- Reports an outage affecting multiple users → check status (Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, Anthropic) and post a status update at brikk.store/status (TODO: build this page)
